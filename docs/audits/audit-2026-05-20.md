## Recommended next steps

**Two findings flagged "critical" warrant clarification before being treated as fixes.** Both relate to `unlockedSolarSystems`. CLAUDE.md §11 and the save-roundtrip-audit baseline document this field as **intentionally NOT persisted** — `hydrate()` re-derives it from `completedMissions ∩ SYSTEM_UNLOCK_GATES` so old saves catch up to gate-map changes without a one-shot migration. The "silent drop" is by design.

The audit subagent did not see that context. Reclassify both to documentation findings unless the gate-derivation contract is changing. If it IS changing (e.g. story-granted unlocks), persist the field via `/new-migration` + the eight save-pipeline layers and run `/save-roundtrip-audit`.

After that triage, the remaining real defect list groups cleanly into five area branches:

- `audit-error-paths` (2 high · 4 medium · 6 low) — the 500-blanket pattern on save + leaderboard, plus the upsert row-count check. Highest robustness ROI because saveQueue currently retries forever on permanent bugs.
- `audit-external-boundaries` (2 high · 6 medium · 7 low) — add rate limits to `/api/save`, `/api/leaderboard`, `/api/handle`; max-body guards on `request.json()`; `.max()` on every Zod array; client-side `AbortSignal.timeout` on every `fetch` consumer.
- `audit-concurrency` (1 high · 3 medium · 3 low) — `usePhaserGame` post-await disposed gate; `useCloudSaveSync` dep-array fix; `useStoryTriggers` interval-id push race; leaderboard route SELECT-then-INSERT inside a transaction.
- `audit-data-integrity` (2 high · 6 medium · 3 low) — `unlockedSolarSystems` triage (see above); remove `as Record<string, unknown>` widening at the DB boundary; tighten `SavePayloadSchema` on the monotonic triplet.
- `audit-resource-lifecycle` (2 medium · 5 low) — fix the two paired rAF leaks in `storyLogAudio.tween` and `story.tweenVolume`; dispose Three.js lights in SceneRig / Sun; clear `combatMusic` watchdog in `stop()`.

Critical/high first, in this order: `audit-error-paths` → `audit-external-boundaries` → `audit-concurrency` → `audit-data-integrity` → `audit-resource-lifecycle`. Work in parallel across branches once the triage of `unlockedSolarSystems` lands, merge with a single `merge:` commit that summarises what landed.
